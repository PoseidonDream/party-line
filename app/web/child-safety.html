Child safety standards

Last updated 5 November 2026 · Party Line (Android app com.poseidondream.partyline, by PoseidonDream)

Party Line has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These standards apply to everything on Party Line: messages, pictures, videos, files, voice messages, sounds, names and profiles, voice channels and screen sharing.

Who can use Party Line

You must be 13 or older to make an account, and everyone confirms this when they sign up. Channels marked 18+ can only be opened by people who have confirmed they are adults.

Strictly forbidden

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind: pictures, videos, drawings, AI-generated images, text or links to it.
  • Grooming: befriending or contacting a minor to sexually abuse or exploit them, online or offline.
  • Sexualising minors, including sexual comments about a child or asking a minor for sexual pictures, messages or calls.
  • Sextortion: threatening a minor, or pressuring them, with intimate pictures or information.
  • Trafficking or offering minors for sexual purposes in any way.

How to report

In the app, point at a message and press the 🚩 flag, or click a person’s name and press Report. Reports go to the admins straight away. You can also Block anyone: they can’t message, call or add you any more. You can also write to the child safety contact below.

What we do

  • Content that breaks these standards is removed as soon as we know about it, and the accounts involved are removed from Party Line.
  • Child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant local authorities, such as the police in the country concerned.
  • We keep what the authorities need for their investigation, as the law requires, and cooperate with them.

The law

Party Line complies with the child safety laws that apply to it, including the laws on reporting child sexual abuse material.

Child safety contact

For anything about child safety on Party Line, ask an admin of this Party Line in the app.
