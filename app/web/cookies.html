Cookie policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Party Line itself sets no cookies and uses no tracking, analytics or advertising. To work, it keeps a few things in your browser’s storage (“local storage”), on your device only. These are strictly necessary, so they don’t need your consent; you can still remove them any time. The only optional part is YouTube, which loads only if you allow it. See also the Privacy policy.

What Party Line stores on your device

NameWhat it’s forHow long
pl:tokenKeeps you signed in on this device.Until you sign out (forgotten by the server after 4 months unused)
pl:settingsYour settings: language, look, microphone, sounds, notifications.Until you remove it
pl:consentYour choice about YouTube in the cookie notice.Until you change it
pl:lastView, pl:lastChan, pl:openDms, pl:closedDms, pl:volsOpening where you left off, which private chats you keep in the list, and each friend’s voice volume.Until you remove them
pl:hadAccount, pl:resetReq, pl:drawTip, pl:skipBuildSmall reminders: showing “Sign in” first, a password reset you asked for, a tip you’ve seen, an app update you skipped.Until you remove them

If you turn on notifications while Party Line is closed, your browser also keeps a notification registration for this site (a service worker). Turning notifications off in Settings removes it.

YouTube (only if you allow it)

“Listen together” in voice and video previews in chats play through YouTube’s player (Google). YouTube can set its own cookies and similar storage, under Google’s privacy policy. Party Line uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode where it can. Until you choose Allow YouTube, nothing loads from YouTube: you see a button to allow it instead.

Changing your choice

In the app: Settings → Account → Privacy → Videos from YouTube. To remove everything Party Line stored on this device, sign out and clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings (on Android: Settings → Apps → Party Line → Storage → Clear data).
