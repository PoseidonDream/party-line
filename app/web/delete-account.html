Delete your account

This deletes your Party Line account on this server and everything that’s yours, for everyone: your messages, private chats, photos, files, voice messages and sounds. Servers you own go to the member who joined first. It happens straight away and can’t be undone. (Copies in the server’s backups disappear within 30 days.)

You can also do this in the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account.
