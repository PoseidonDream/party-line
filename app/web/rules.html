Community rules

Last updated 29 October 2026

Party Line is for hanging out with friends. By making an account you agree to these rules. They apply to everything you post: messages, pictures, videos, files, voice messages, sounds, your name and your profile, and to how you behave in voice.

Not allowed

  • Harassment and bullying: threatening, stalking, or ganging up on anyone, or posting things to humiliate them.
  • Hate: attacking people for their race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar.
  • Violence: threats, encouraging violence or self-harm, or praising terrorism.
  • Sexual content involving minors of any kind. It is reported to the authorities.
  • Sexual content shared with people who didn’t ask for it, and intimate pictures of anyone without their consent.
  • Spam and scams: advertising, chain messages, phishing links or malware.
  • Illegal things: selling drugs or weapons, or sharing things you don’t have the right to share.
  • Pretending to be someone else to trick people.
  • Sharing someone’s private information (address, phone number, private pictures) without their permission.

If you see something

Report it: point at the message and press the 🚩 flag, or click the person’s name and press Report. The admins get it straight away. You can also Block anyone: you won’t see their messages, and they can’t message, call or add you.

What admins can do

Admins and server owners can delete messages, mute or disconnect people from voice, and remove them from servers. Breaking these rules can get your messages deleted and your account removed.

Questions: ask an admin of this Party Line in the app.
